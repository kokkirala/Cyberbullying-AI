CYBERBULLYING REPORTING

If you experience cyberbullying or online harassment:

1. Save evidence.
   Take screenshots and save relevant messages, usernames, URLs, dates, and other information.

2. Do not retaliate.
   Avoid responding with threats, insults, or harassment.

3. Block or mute the account.
   Use blocking or muting features to reduce unwanted contact.

4. Report the content.
   Use the platform's official reporting system and select the category that best matches the behavior.

5. Tell someone you trust.
   Consider talking to a parent, teacher, friend, counselor, or another trusted person.

6. Protect your account.
   Review privacy settings, use a strong password, and enable multi-factor authentication when available.

7. Take serious threats seriously.
   If someone makes credible threats of violence, stalking, extortion, or other serious danger, seek appropriate professional or local-authority assistance.

8. Keep records.
   Do not delete important evidence before saving copies of it.

AI systems can provide general safety guidance, but they cannot determine whether conduct violates a specific law or platform policy.
