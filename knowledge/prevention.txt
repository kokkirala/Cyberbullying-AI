CYBERBULLYING PREVENTION

1. Protect personal information.
   Avoid publicly sharing addresses, phone numbers, passwords, private photographs, or other sensitive information.

2. Use privacy settings.
   Review social media privacy settings and limit who can contact you or see your content.

3. Use strong account security.
   Use strong unique passwords and enable multi-factor authentication when available.

4. Avoid retaliating.
   Do not respond with threats, insults, or harassment. Retaliation can escalate the situation.

5. Block or mute harmful accounts.
   Blocking or muting can reduce unwanted contact and harassment.

6. Save evidence.
   Keep screenshots, URLs, usernames, dates, and other relevant information when harassment occurs.

7. Report abusive behavior.
   Use the platform's reporting system when content violates its rules.

8. Support people who are being targeted.
   Do not participate in harassment. Offer support and encourage the person to report the behavior.

9. Talk to a trusted person.
   Parents, teachers, friends, counselors, or other trusted people can provide support.

10. Escalate serious situations.
    If messages contain credible threats of violence, stalking, extortion, or other serious danger, seek appropriate professional or local-authority assistance.
